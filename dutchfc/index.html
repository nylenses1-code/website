How It Works

Four quick steps. You do not pay until NY Lenses confirms your selected photos and total.

1

Open the gallery

Tap “View Gallery” and find the photos you want.

2

Tap the hearts

Tap the heart on each photo. Then tap the heart at the top, open your list, tap Share, and copy the link.

3

Text your order

Tap the button below and paste your Favorites link. Fill in the player, team, and package.

4

Pay and receive

NY Lenses confirms the total. After payment, your high-resolution photos are sent to you.
