Elite Game-Day Photo Program

A proposed one-event parent-pay photography pilot for Elite Leagues & Sports. NY Lenses covers the action, families choose whether to purchase, and Elite pays no upfront photography fee.

$0UPFRONT

Photography pilot

One event to test participation, parent interest, communication, and delivery before discussing a larger schedule.

  • Parent-purchase photo gallery
  • Ten club-use highlight images included
  • Video available as a paid add-on
The proposed tradeoff

Lower organizational cost. Simple access for families.

Instead of Elite paying the standard event photography rate, NY Lenses would photograph one approved pilot event and offer optional digital photo packages directly to participating families.

01

NY Lenses photographs

Approved games or event activity are photographed with a focus on usable player action, team moments, and event atmosphere.

02

Parents choose

Edited, watermarked previews are posted in an online gallery. Buying is completely optional, and families select only the photos they want.

03

Elite receives highlights

Elite receives ten edited images from the pilot for its own website, social media, registration, and league promotion under a non-exclusive license.

What Elite provides
  • Event approval, schedule, venue access, and applicable youth-photo requirements
  • Promotion of the QR/gallery to participating families before and after the event
  • A point of contact for event-day coordination
Parent experience

Four straightforward steps

The ordering process is designed for phones and requires no purchase from Elite Leagues & Sports.

01

Scan

Families open the event page from the shared QR code.

02

Browse

Parents find their athlete in the watermarked online gallery.

03

Select

They heart their favorite images and share the photo numbers.

04

Receive

After payment is confirmed, clean high-resolution files are delivered digitally.

Pilot terms

Clear before the first event

Approval and youth-photo policies

Coverage begins only after Elite confirms the event, venue access, and any consent, credential, privacy, or youth-photography requirements.

Copyright and club use

NY Lenses retains copyright. Elite receives a non-exclusive promotional-use license for the ten delivered pilot highlight images.

No guaranteed sales

Parent purchases are optional, and no sales volume or revenue result is promised.

Future coverage

After the pilot, both sides can review participation and decide whether to schedule additional event, tournament, or season coverage.
